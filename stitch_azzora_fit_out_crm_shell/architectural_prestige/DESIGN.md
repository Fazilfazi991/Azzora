## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an ultra-premium, architectural design and fit-out CRM environment tailored for luxury residential, penthouse, hospitality, and corporate real estate spaces in the UAE and Dubai. It departs radically from disposable SaaS clichés—eliminating vivid electric blues, cold purple gradients, heavy neomorphism, and hazy glass blurs in favor of a timeless, tactile, and highly disciplined architectural atmosphere.

Drawing deeply from high-end architectural monographs, bespoke materiality boards, and structural joinery drawings, the style blends **Warm Architectural Minimalism** with **Constructivist Precision**. The emotional cadence is unhurried, commanding, discreet, and rigorously engineered. Interfaces mirror the quality of hand-cast plaster, polished travertine, patinated bronze hardware, and architectural linen, reassuring project stakeholders, high-net-worth clients, and master contractors of absolute precision, craftsmanship, and operational excellence.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in mineral substrates, kiln-fired architectural clay, and desert metals, directly drawn from the brand emblem's structural terracotta tone.

### Palette Hierarchy & Intent
- **Canvas & Backdrops:** Warm alabaster (`#F9F8F5`) and foundational ivory limestone (`#F5F3EE`) form the resting background, mitigating harsh digital glare while communicating quiet opulence.
- **Surfaces & Cards:** Crisp chalk white (`#FFFFFF`) layered with warm architectural cream (`#FAF9F6`), framed by structural 1px stone hairline borders (`#E8E5DF`, `#DFDBD3`).
- **Primary Mineral Tone:** Rich Terracotta / Architectural Rust (`#9A3C25`, hover: `#883520`), taken directly from the mark's faceted geometry. Used purposefully for primary CTAs, active milestone states, and authoritative focal anchors.
- **Secondary Alloy Accent:** Desert Sand & Brushed Warm Bronze (`#C2A27A`, deep: `#A68A64`), reserved for executive tier badges, project tier tags, subtle highlights, and metalwork metadata.
- **Typography & Structural Contrast:** Espresso Charcoal (`#1A1918`) for high-contrast primary content; softened deep stone (`#2B2927`) and architectural sand-gray (`#6B6661`, `#8C867F`) for secondary labels and specifications.
- **Architectural Status System:**
  - *Approved / On Track:* Patinated Sage Green (`#4E7058`, soft wash: `#F0F4F1`)
  - *Pending / Under Review:* Warm Muted Ochre (`#B88334`, soft wash: `#FBF6EE`)
  - *Critical / Variance / Delay:* Earth Terracotta Red (`#A33A2B`, soft wash: `#FAF1EF`)

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the structured, geometric drafting-table authority of **Space Grotesk** with the neutral, hyper-legible structural clarity of **Inter**.

- **Display & Headlines (Space Grotesk):** Evokes mid-century architectural manifests, construction drawings, and high-fashion engineering monographs. Numeric figures are clean, monolinear, and robust for contract values, square meterage, and BoQ data.
- **Body & Data Content (Inter):** Applied across all dense CRM records, site notes, material specifications, and communication logs. It guarantees frictionless readability at small scales against warm textured backdrops.
- **Architectural Labels & Sub-headings:** Set strictly in Space Grotesk uppercase with tracked spacing (`+0.08em` to `+0.12em`). Category demarcations, structural stages (e.g., `MEP ROUGH-IN`, `JOINERY FABRICATION`, `SITE HANDOVER`), and breadcrumbs strictly adopt this convention to mirror technical architectural blueprints.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is governed by a disciplined **8px baseline rhythm** reminiscent of classic architectural proportions and modular construction grids.

### Grid & Composition
- **Desktop (1440px+):** A 12-column structural grid anchored by a 280px fixed architectural side rail (project directory and phase navigator). Gutters remain consistent at `1.5rem` (24px) with outer canvas margins of `2.5rem` (40px) to afford ample negative space, conveying luxury through restraint.
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px):** 8-column layout with 24px gutters and 24px margins. Sub-dashboards and milestone Gantt views reflow into stacked dimensional modules.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid layout with `1rem` (16px) gutters and `1.25rem` (20px) margins. Critical project approvals, drawing inspections, and site photos prioritize vertical cadence over complex multi-column matrices.

### Spacing Density
Spacing tokens operate as modular intervals:
- `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px): Micro-gaps between status pips, technical icons, and tracked uppercase badges.
- `space-md` (16px): Standard inner card padding and input interior gaps.
- `space-lg` (24px): Card module padding, section separations within modal drawers.
- `space-xl` (40px): Broad whitespace separating distinct operational zones (e.g., Site Timeline vs. Financial Procurement Summaries).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on synthetic drop shadows or glossy surfaces. Instead, depth is conveyed through **tectonic material layering** and **ambient low-contrast outlines**.

1. **Substrate (Base Layer):** Foundational ivory (`#F9F8F5`), establishing an earthy, warm ambient foundation.
2. **Structural Slabs (Cards & Panels):** Chalk white (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px perimeter border rendered in refined limestone gray (`#E8E5DF`).
3. **Hover & Interactive Rise:** Elevated cards do not cast dramatic black shadows. They shift with an ultra-diffused, warm umber micro-shadow:
   - `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(43, 41, 39, 0.05), 0 1px 3px rgba(43, 41, 39, 0.03);`
   - Border subtly shifts from `#E8E5DF` to `#DFDBD3`.
4. **Modals & Flyouts:** Floating specification drawers and CAD viewers use a deep-stacked border with an ambient architectural vignette:
   - `box-shadow: 0 20px 48px -12px rgba(26, 25, 24, 0.08), 0 0 0 1px rgba(223, 219, 211, 0.8);`
5. **Backdrop Overlays:** Dimming overlays use semi-opaque warm charcoal (`rgba(26, 25, 24, 0.45)`) combined with a crisp 4px Gaussian blur, evoking a linen drafting veil.

## Shapes

The geometric silhouette references the beveled and chamfered architectural language of the brand mark. While standard UI components maintain a controlled radius to ensure interface usability, corner treatments never feel bubbly or toy-like.

- **Standard Elements (Inputs, Buttons, Cards):** `roundedness: 2` (0.5rem / 8px to 10px corner radius), expressing precision-milled joinery.
- **Containers & Project Panels:** 12px (`rounded-lg`), providing soft framing to CAD drawings, material renders, and site logs without compromising architectural rigidity.
- **Status Indicators & Micro Chips:** 4px to 6px (`rounded-sm`), rejecting generic full-pill shapes in favor of calibrated architectural plate geometries.
- **Emblematic Accent Features:** Key milestone cards and hero header banners may incorporate subtle micro-chamfers (clipped 45-degree corner details) inspired directly by the angular facets of the emblem.

- **Base Style:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#E8E5DF`, radius 8px, height 44px, padding 0 16px. Typography Inter 14px `#1A1918`. Placeholder `#8C867F`.
- **Focus State:** Border shifts to `#9A3C25` with a subtle outline ring: `0 0 0 1px #9A3C25`. No generic glowing halos.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- **Checkboxes:** 18px × 18px square with 4px radius. Border 1.5px solid `#DFDBD3`. Checked state fills `#9A3C25` with a crisp chalk white architectural tick mark.
- **Radio Buttons:** 18px round with 1.5px solid `#DFDBD3`. Selected state displays an inner filled bronze disc (`#9A3C25`) inset by 4px.

### Cards & Site Data Panels
- **Container Structure:** Background `#FFFFFF`, 1px solid `#E8E5DF`, radius 12px, padding 24px.
- **Header:** Uppercase tracked project code (e.g., `AZ-DUB-PALM-402`) in Space Grotesk 11px `#8C867F`, headline in Space Grotesk 20px `#1A1918`.
- **Data Tables:** Zebra-striping avoided; rows separated by 1px hairline rules `#F5F3EE`. Numeric financial and dimension data tabularly aligned in Inter Mono or Space Grotesk.

- **Materiality & Finishes Drawer:** Dual-column view pairing high-res texture photography with structured technical specifications (supplier, batch reference, installation sign-off).
